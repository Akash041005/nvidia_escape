# 🏃‍♂️ Nvidia Escape: Silicon Valley Run
### *3D Endless Runner Web Game — Subway Surfers Parody*

A full-fledged, high-performance 3D endless runner parody inspired by *Subway Surfers*, built using **Next.js (App Router)**, **Three.js**, **Tailwind CSS**, and the browser's **Web Audio API**.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

Open your browser and navigate to:
👉 **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)**

### 🕹️ Controls:
- **`←` / `→` or `A` / `D`**: Switch Lanes (Left, Center, Right)
- **`↑` / `Space` or `W`**: Jump over low barriers, news van hoods & collect wafer arcs
- **`↓` or `S`**: Duck / Slide under Lori's $75B divorce banners
- **Mobile Touch**: Swipe Left / Right / Up / Down, or use on-screen touch buttons

---

## 🌟 Features & Parody Mechanics

### 1. The Characters & Chaser Mechanics
- **Jensen Huang (Runner)**: Chibi 3D model featuring white hair spikes, black-rim glasses, iconic black leather biker jacket with metallic zippers, and denim jeans. Fully animated sprint, jump, duck, and flight cycles.
- **Chinese PM (Hunter/Chaser)**: Chasing Jensen from behind in a formal dark suit and red tie. Stumbling on an obstacle causes him to close in immediately! A second stumble while he is on your heels triggers capture and **Game Over**.

### 2. Obstacles
- **Media Broadcast Vans**: Blocky news vans featuring **CNBC BREAKING**, **BLOOMBERG LIVE**, and **WSJ NEWS** dynamic scrolling LED ticker textures. Includes **Ramp Vans** that let you leap onto roofs and roof-run!
- **Nvidia Board Members**: Angry executives holding wide placards reading **"YOU'RE FIRED!"** (Jump or dodge).
- **Romantic Scandals / Affairs**: Characters holding neon placards reading **"WE WANT JUSTICE! 💔"**.
- **Wife Lori (Legal Barrier)**: High legal banner reading **"I WANT DIVORCE - $75 BILLION ⚖️"** (Slide underneath!).

### 3. Collectibles & Live Stock Market HUD
- **Silicon Wafers**: Glowing rainbow holographic semiconductor discs (+`$2.50` stock price each).
- **Live Stock Ticker HUD**: Real-time NASDAQ: NVDA price ticker with live animated SVG candlestick/line chart.
- **Delisting Risk**: Severe stumbles drop stock by `-$15.00`. If stock hits `$0.00`, Jensen is delisted under Chapter 11 bankruptcy!

### 4. Cameo Power-Ups
- **Elon Musk SpaceX Rocket Boost (8s)**: Mounts a flaming SpaceX Falcon rocket booster onto Jensen's back, soaring high above all obstacles while raining dense wafer showers across all three lanes.
- **Donald Trump Tariff & Bailout Shield (10s)**: Surrounds Jensen in a golden glowing forcefield with a floating *"★ FAKE NEWS! PROTECTED! ★"* banner, blasting through obstacles for bonus stock points!

### 5. 3D Model Download (.GLB)
- You don't need to hunt for external broken 3D models online! Every character, van, and prop is procedurally sculpted in Three.js with custom geometries and textures.
- A **"Download 3D Jensen Model (.GLB)"** button is built right into the Start and Game Over screens so you can export and keep the 3D model file directly on your computer!

### 6. Procedural Audio
- 100% synthesized in real-time via the browser's Web Audio API: coin chimes, jump whooshes, slide sweeps, crash booms, rocket thrusters, shield fanfares, and an upbeat synthwave background music loop.
